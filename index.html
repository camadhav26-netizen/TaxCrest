For salaried individuals: Form 16, bank statements, investment proofs (80C/80D), and PAN/Aadhaar. For business income: P&L statement, balance sheet, bank statements, GST returns, and TDS certificates. I'll guide you on exactly what's required for your specific case.
CMA (Credit Monitoring Arrangement) Data is a detailed financial report banks require for processing working capital loans (CC/OD) and term loans above a certain limit. It includes past financials, projected profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow, fund flow, and key financial ratios. Most banks require it for loans above ₹10 lakh.
GST registration is typically completed within 3–7 working days after all documents are submitted. Required documents include PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, bank account details, and business proof. I handle the entire application process on your behalf.
